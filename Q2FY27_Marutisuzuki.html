Which parts are imported?

Largest

Electronics

  • Chips and ECU internals
  • Sensors, ADAS radar/camera
  • Infotainment, display modules

Vendors such as Denso assemble here using imported chips.

EV / hybrid

Battery & motor

  • LFP cells for e Vitara (imported)
  • Strong-hybrid system parts
  • Rare-earth magnets for motors (China)

Hybrid cells made at TDSG Gujarat — local manufacturing with imported materials.

Precision

Powertrain parts

  • Fuel-injection precision parts
  • Automatic transmission parts
  • Some special steels and alloys
  • Natural hedge: exports of Fronx, Jimny and e Vitara to Japan crossed 1 lakh units.
  • Forex took 40 bps off margin in Q2 (QoQ).

2. Royalty — the history

  • Royalty pays Suzuki for model design, engines and technology.
  • FY08 2.7% → FY13 5.75% (incl. Fiat diesel) — minority holders objected.
  • In-house R&D brought it down to ~3.5% (FY25).
  • New models since Brezza (2016) pay royalty in rupees — less yen risk.
  • FY26 ~₹6,100 cr and Q2 FY27 ~₹1,770 cr are CB estimates (3.5% × sales).

Why is Maruti still No. 1?

Share slid from 51% (FY20) to 38.9% (FY26), and is now back at 40.8%.

1. Reach
  • 6,000+ service points in 3,000+ towns
  • Two retail channels: Arena + Nexa
2. Lowest running cost
  • Mileage and cheap spares
  • Largest CNG range
3. Every price point
  • ₹4 lakh to ₹25 lakh
  • Biggest gainer of the small-car revival
4. Resale and trust
  • Strong resale value, True Value
  • Default choice for first-time buyers
5. Scale and localisation
  • ~24 lakh cars a year keep unit cost low
  • ~96% of parts local
6. SUV comeback
  • Brezza, Fronx, Grand Vitara, Victoris
  • SUV sales up 68% in Q2

Weak spot: Tata, M&M and MG lead in EVs; Maruti has no diesel.

5 key takeaways

1 · Demand isn't the problem
  • Record volume, 2.7 lakh pending orders, 17 days of stock.
2 · Margin is the question
  • 12.7% → 9.4% on steel, copper and forex.
3 · Part of the drop is non-operating
  • Treasury MTM loss — can reverse if yields ease.
4 · Japan's share is large
  • Royalty ~3.5% of sales ≈ 73% of Q2 profit.
5 · The capacity race is on
  • 29 → 40 lakh; ₹77,500 cr capex till FY31.

How Maruti got there
  • 1983: Maruti 800 — a Government of India + Suzuki JV.
  • Began with 33% local parts → 95%+ localisation.
  • 2002: Suzuki subsidiary; 2015: Nexa.
  • Lost share in the SUV wave; won it back with Brezza, Fronx, Grand Vitara.
  • 2025 GST cut revived small cars; share back to 40.8%.

India's car need and the value chain

  • FY26 passenger-vehicle sales: 46.8 lakh (+8.4%).
  • India has only ~30 cars per 1,000 people — lots of headroom.
  • GST: 18% on small cars, 40% on bigger ones — affordability reset.
  • CAFE norms force a hybrid/CNG/EV mix.

CB analyst view

  • Business: demand, share and capacity are strong — Q2 was a record volume quarter.
  • Earnings: hit by commodities and MTM; operating margin improved over Q1.
  • Japan link: royalty at ~3.5% of sales is the key cost for minority holders to track.

  • Top positive triggers: H2 order book, capacity ramp. Top risk: steel/copper.
  • Watch: whether Q3 margin holds above 9.4% and exports recover.
