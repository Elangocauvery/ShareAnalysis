Eternal (with Swiggy & Paytm) — Q2 FY27 Business & Investment Analysis

Quarter ended 30 Sep 2026 · Eternal ₹312.35 (21 Oct) · Swiggy ₹186 · Paytm ₹1,655 (26 Oct close) · ₹ crore unless stated

Overview — the quarter in one look

Eternal grew like a rocket but earned like a bank this quarter: the ₹351 cr it earned on its cash pile (other income) was larger than its entire ₹323 cr pre-tax profit. Take that away and the operating business lost ₹28 cr before tax — but that core loss is the smallest in three years (it was −₹223 cr a year ago), so the operating engine is closing in on standing on its own feet.

    Business model — how Eternal makes (and keeps) money

    Business model in 60 seconds: Eternal runs four consumer apps on one delivery network. Zomato takes a commission and fees on restaurant orders. Blinkit now mostly buys goods and sells them from 2,751 dark stores in about 10 minutes, so the full price of the goods shows up as revenue. District sells movie, event and dining tickets. Hyperpure supplies restaurants. A large cash pile parked in deposits and bonds earns interest, which shows up as "other income".

    Business model journey — from listing (Jul 2021) to Q2 FY27

    At listing, Zomato was an asset-light food-delivery marketplace: it never owned the food, it took a cut, and restaurants and customers' money flowed through it before it paid out (negative working capital). Five years later Eternal is mainly a quick-commerce retailer that owns inventory, runs thousands of dark stores and carries big depreciation and lease bills. The highlighted changes below explain why revenue jumped, why margins look thin and why cash is now being used.

    Profit anatomy — more from other income than from operations

    In Q2FY27, other income (₹351 cr) was 109% of profit before tax (₹323 cr). The operating business on its own was at −₹28 cr before tax. This has been true in almost every quarter since Zomato turned profitable — interest on the IPO and QIP money has been carrying the bottom line.

    Cash reserves — the war chest

    Eternal closed Q2FY27 with ₹16,475 cr of cash, deposits and liquid investments — down ₹1,813 cr in the quarter as Blinkit stocked up for the festive season and opened 308 stores (capex ₹941 cr). This pile, built from the 2021 IPO and the ₹8,500 cr QIP of Nov 2024, earns roughly 8% a year, which is exactly the ₹351 cr other income that props up profit. Every rupee spent on stores and inventory therefore also lowers future other income.

    Cash flow & working capital

    The working-capital story is the business-model change in numbers. As a marketplace, Zomato collected money from customers and paid restaurants later, so suppliers funded it (cash conversion cycle of −513 days in FY21). Owning Blinkit inventory flipped this: inventory days rose to 26 and payable days fell to 36, so the cycle is now about +2 days. Growth now needs cash.
