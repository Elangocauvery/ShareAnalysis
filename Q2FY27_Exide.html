Why is LFP so hard to make in India?India-ல LFP செய்யறது ஏன் இவ்வளவு கஷ்டம்?

Exide can assemble the cell, but most of what goes inside it still comes from China.Exide cell-ஐ செய்யலாம், ஆனா உள்ள போற பொருள் பெரும்பாலும் இன்னும் China-ல இருந்து தான்.

"Himadri and others are making it — so why is it still hard?""Himadri மாதிரி company செய்யுறாங்களே — அப்புறம் ஏன் கஷ்டம்?"

  • Making cathode in India ≠ materials from India: Himadri and Altmin still need imported lithium carbonate; India has no lithium refining yet.
  • A cell maker must test a new cathode or anode supplier for months before switching — and Exide's licensed SVOLT cell designs were built around the partner's supply chain.
  • Exide's plan: localise electrolyte first, then 50–60% of the bill of materials in 2–3 years as Indian suppliers qualify.
  • Watch 10 Nov 2026: China's suspension of its Oct 2025 battery-material curbs is documented to that date. Its July 2025 curbs on LFP-cathode technology are still in force.
  • India-ல cathode செய்யுறது ≠ India பொருள்: Himadri, Altmin-க்கும் lithium carbonate import தான்; India-ல lithium refining இன்னும் இல்ல.
  • புது cathode/anode supplier-ஐ மாத்தணும்னா cell maker மாசக்கணக்குல test பண்ணணும் — Exide-ஓட SVOLT licence cell design partner supply chain-ஐ வச்சு உருவானது.
  • Exide plan: முதல்ல electrolyte local, அப்புறம் 2–3 வருஷத்துல bill of materials-ல 50–60%, Indian suppliers qualify ஆக ஆக.
  • 10 Nov 2026 கவனிங்க: China Oct 2025 battery-material கட்டுப்பாடு suspension அந்த தேதி வரை தான். July 2025 LFP-cathode technology கட்டுப்பாடு இன்னும் அமல்ல.

Sector need & value chain — where Exide playsSector தேவை & value chain — Exide எங்க இருக்கு

  • Lead-acid: Exide is present end to end — recycling-linked lead supply, plates, batteries, dealer network.
  • Li-ion: Exide now makes cells (EESL) and packs; cathode, anode, separator and most electrolyte are bought.
  • Need: India's domestic cell supply was ~2 GWh at end-2025 vs ~17 GWh demand, and a ~260 GWh storage tender pipeline.
  • Grid storage (BESS) is the fastest-growing user of LFP: India added 8.2 GWh of storage in H1 2026.
  • Lead-acid: Exide முழு chain-லயும் இருக்கு — lead supply, plates, battery, dealer network.
