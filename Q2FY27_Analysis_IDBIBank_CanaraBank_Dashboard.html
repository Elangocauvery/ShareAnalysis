Quarter at a glance காலாண்டு சுருக்கம்

Read-out. Loans grew 22% YoY to ₹2,58,968 crore while deposits grew only 10%, pushing the CD ratio to 79.50%. NIM slipped to 3.61% (−54 bps QoQ) and other income fell 28%, so operating profit fell 8%. PAT still rose 5% to ₹2,115 crore because of a ₹637 crore provision write-back. Asset quality and capital remain best-in-class: GNPA 2.30%, NNPA 0.16%, PCR 99.31%, CRAR 26.92%. கடன் 22% வளர்ந்தது, டெபாசிட் 10% தான். NIM 3.61%-க்கு குறைஞ்சது, மற்ற வருமானம் 28% குறைஞ்சது. ஆனா ₹637 கோடி provision write-back-ஆல PAT 5% உயர்ந்து ₹2,115 கோடி. சொத்து தரம், மூலதனம் ரொம்ப வலுவா இருக்கு.

Funding & liquidity (the bank's version of working capital)

Banks do not carry inventory or receivable days. The equivalent question is whether loans are funded by stable, cheap deposits. The CD ratio jumped from 71.4% to 79.5% in a year because loans grew more than twice as fast as deposits. That uses up idle liquidity and is one reason NIM is under pressure: new deposits have to be raised at market rates. LCR was not disclosed in the sources reviewed.

CB School — how the metrics connect CB பள்ளி

High CRAR lowers ROE. IDBI's 26.92% CRAR means lots of equity per rupee of loans. Profit is spread over a bigger equity base, so ROE (14.80%) trails ROA (1.89%) by less leverage than peers. Fairfax will likely try to deploy this capital.
Rate cuts squeeze NIM first. Loans linked to repo reprice down immediately; fixed deposits reprice only at renewal. Yield fell to 8.46% while deposit cost fell to 4.59% — the gap narrows for a few quarters.
Write-backs lift PAT, not operating strength. Operating profit fell 8%, yet PAT rose 5% because provisions turned into a ₹637 crore release. Look at operating profit to judge core earnings.
CD ratio is a liquidity gauge. At 79.5%, ₹79.50 of every ₹100 deposited is lent. Rising CD ratio means growth must now be funded by new, costlier deposits.
PCR near 100%. Almost every bad loan is already provided for, so future recoveries can flow back as write-backs — the reason provisions were negative this quarter.
P/BV is the bank valuation. At ₹90.39 the market pays 1.65x book. Fairfax pays 1.48x book for control — a reminder that market price and deal value can differ.

CB School — Canara Bank lessons

Rising bond yields hurt treasury. Banks hold government bonds. When yields rise 44 bps, bond prices fall, creating a mark-to-market loss. Canara's treasury income fell from ₹1,583 crore to ₹499 crore.
FCNR deposits: cheap but not CASA. Foreign-currency deposits cost about 1% less than bulk deposits, cutting cost of funds, but they are term deposits, so the CASA ratio fell to 29%.
What is ECL? Expected Credit Loss provisioning makes banks provide for losses they expect, not only losses already incurred. The switch front-loads provisions — ₹12,000–15,000 crore for Canara.
Same ROA, different ROE. Canara ROA 1.04% gives ROE 18.49%; IDBI ROA 1.89% gives ROE 14.80%. Canara runs with far more leverage (CRAR 17.19% vs 26.92%).
Why 1x book vs 1.65x? IDBI carries a takeover premium and higher NIM; Canara has low NIM and the ECL overhang, so the market pays about book value.
Operating profit vs PAT. Canara's operating profit grew only 2% but PAT grew 8.4% because provisions fell 6.1%.
